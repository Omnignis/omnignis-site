Services

What we do.

Omnignis is a security-first managed service provider. One monthly agreement covers your security and your IT: on-site onboarding, managed protection, a helpdesk that answers, and the reporting that proves it. Plus custom software on request, and a specialized reporting tool for churches.

01 · Onboard & Harden

We start where the wires are

Every engagement begins on site. Remote tools tell you what a network claims to be; the wiring closet tells you what it is. We map it, fix the structure, and install what's missing.

  • On-site assessment. Accounts, devices, email, network, and the physical layout, delivered as a plain-English report with a prioritized fix list. For healthcare practices, this is the HIPAA Security Risk Analysis federal law already requires.
  • Hardware where it's needed. An OPNsense firewall, business-grade access points, and a backup appliance, installed and configured. You own the hardware. We manage it.
  • Network segmentation. Guest wifi, business systems, and your most sensitive equipment separated, so one infected laptop can't reach everything.
  • Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace hardening. MFA enforced properly, legacy sign-ins closed, mail rules audited, forwarding locked down, where most real attacks start and end.
  • Email authentication & anti-fraud. SPF, DKIM, and DMARC done all the way, so criminals can't send invoices as you, plus the payment-change process rule that stops wire fraud cold.
  • A management agent on every endpoint. Secure remote access for monitoring and repair that doesn't depend on your firewall to let us in.
02 · Managed Protection

Then we run it, month after month

The monthly program. Detection, maintenance, and proof, delivered for a flat rate and summarized in a report you can hand to your insurance carrier, regulator, or board.

  • Remote monitoring & management (RMM). Continuous visibility into every endpoint: health, patching, and problems caught before they become outages.
  • Endpoint detection & response (EDR). The actual threat detection. Malicious behavior gets caught and the machine isolated as it happens, not discovered later in the damage.
  • Encrypted backup & recovery. Physical, encrypted backups with restores we actually test, so ransomware is an inconvenience instead of an ending.
  • Email security & phishing simulation. Filtering and fraud controls on the mail itself, plus periodic phishing tests that keep your team sharp.
  • Security awareness training. Short, practical sessions on phishing and payment fraud. Meets Texas HB 300 training requirements for healthcare workplaces.
  • Compliance reporting. A monthly record of what we watched, what we fixed, and where you stand: the client-facing evidence carriers and regulators ask for.
  • Quarterly reviews & renewals. Account and access reviews every quarter, and your cyber-insurance questionnaire answered accurately at renewal.
03 · Helpdesk

A person answers

Managed clients get one number for anything technical, from a locked account to a printer that won't print. No phone tree, no ticket black hole, and honest hours in writing.

  • Live helpdesk, Monday through Friday, 8am to 1pm Central. Call or email, a person picks it up.
  • After hours: next-business-day response. Stated in your agreement, not implied and quietly missed.
  • Escalation to the engineers who built your network. The person who fixes your problem is the person who knows your setup.
  • Security alerts run on their own track. Detections are triaged under the managed protection program, separate from the helpdesk queue.
04 · Custom Software (On Request)

When the right tool doesn't exist, we build it

Omnignis is a security practice first. But when a client needs internal tooling (a portal, an automation, a report generator), we build it ourselves, secure by default, rather than referring it out.

  • Small business websites. Designed, built, and launched: fast, secure, and easy to keep updated.
  • Custom web apps & client portals. Logins, dashboards, and tools shaped around how your business runs.
  • Workflow automation & reporting. The work you do by hand every week, made automatic: your data pulled, formatted, and emailed on a schedule.

What we don't do

A security company you can trust should be clear about where its job ends. These three things are outside our scope, on purpose.

Not offered

Cameras, alarms & door access

Physical security systems are licensed work in Texas. We don't sell, install, service, monitor, or respond to them, and we'll refer you to a licensed integrator who does.

Not offered

24/7 emergency response

We won't promise coverage we can't deliver. Our contracts say business hours and next-business-day, and we keep a vetted incident-response firm on file for true emergencies.

Not offered

Investigations of people

Finding out who did something is licensed private-investigation work in Texas. We handle the technical side and refer attribution questions to a licensed investigator.
