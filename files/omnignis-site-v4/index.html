How small businesses actually lose money

The pattern, start to finish

Someone on your team gets a convincing email and types their password into a fake login page. The attacker signs into that mailbox and sits there quietly, reading, for three weeks. They learn who pays whom, what invoices look like, who approves what.

Then they email your bookkeeper a real-looking invoice with new banking details. The money leaves. Nobody notices for a month.

A firewall doesn't stop that. Neither does a VPN or an antivirus box. Identity controls, mail controls, detection, and one boring process rule do. That's what we run.

The stack

Remote monitoring & management

Every computer runs our agent, so we see problems early, patch on schedule, and fix most issues remotely, without depending on your firewall to let us in.

Endpoint detection & response

The actual threat detection. Malicious behavior on any machine gets caught and isolated as it happens, not discovered a month later in the damage.

Email security & phishing simulation

Hardened mail rules, locked-down forwarding, and periodic phishing tests, so the convincing fake invoice gets caught by both people and machines.

Encrypted backup & recovery

Physical, encrypted backups that we prove restore. Ransomware becomes an inconvenience when your data safely exists somewhere the attacker can't reach.

Security awareness training

Short, practical sessions your team will actually remember: phishing, payment fraud, and the habits that stop most attacks. Meets Texas HB 300 for healthcare workplaces.

Compliance reporting

A monthly record of what we watched, what we fixed, and where you stand. The evidence your carrier, regulator, or diocese asks for, already written.

What we don't do

A security company you can trust should be clear about where its job ends. These three things are outside our scope, on purpose.

Not offered

Cameras, alarms & door access

Physical security systems are licensed work in Texas. We don't sell, install, service, monitor, or respond to them, and we'll refer you to a licensed integrator who does.

Not offered

24/7 emergency response

We won't promise coverage we can't deliver. Our contracts say business hours and next-business-day, and we keep a vetted incident-response firm on file for true emergencies.

Not offered

Investigations of people

Finding out who did something is licensed private-investigation work in Texas. We handle the technical side and refer attribution questions to a licensed investigator.

About Omnignis
